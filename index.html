Perfect Fit

by M. A. Harrington

Cynthia, a therapist, meets Sam, a park ranger, at a speed dating event. They build a life together and welcome a daughter, Lucy — but when Lucy is diagnosed with CDKL5, everything changes. This is a story about love, loss, and fighting for the world to see a child, not just a diagnosis.
